The Process

How it will work.

Three simple steps, built around New York State facility rules from day one.

01

Browse Approved Items

Only items that meet facility rules will show up to choose from. No dead ends, no guessing what’s allowed.

02

We Handle Compliance

Every package is assembled to meet DOCCS Directive 4911 and 4911A packaging rules, so it isn’t rejected at intake.

03

Shipped Direct

Once approved, your package is sent straight to the facility your loved one is housed at.

Why This Matters

Why we’re building this.

Sending a package to someone inside is confusing and easy to get wrong. Facility rules vary, requirements change, and one mistake — the wrong brand, the wrong packaging, an item that isn’t on the approved list — can get the whole package rejected. That’s a real cost to families who are already carrying a lot. We’re building a simple, compliant service so care packages make it to the people they’re meant for.

A package that makes it through is one more way to say: I’m still here.
Compliance First

Built the right way, before we open

This service follows New York State DOCCS Directive 4911 and 4911A. It isn’t live yet. We’re building it carefully, the right way, before we open it to families.
