The Characters

Meet the pack

Two lovable NYC dogs, two very different outlooks, and a friendship that carries the whole story.

🐕

Jiggs

Jiggs is curious and a little scrappy, always first to explore whatever's around the next corner. Every block of the city looks like a new adventure to him.

🐩

Glo

Warm and steady, Glo is quick with encouragement. She's the friend who makes the big city feel smaller and a lot friendlier for everyone around her.

The Series

About the series

Book One of Jiggs & Glo is finished, and it has the same heart as everything else we do. More Jiggs and Glo stories are already on the way, each one built around a simple idea: friendship and a little bravery can make even the biggest city feel like home.
