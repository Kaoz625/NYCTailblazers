The Characters

Meet the real Jiggs & Glo.

The series is inspired by two real NYC dogs. These are their actual photos — the storybook versions are drawn after them.

Jiggs

Curious & a little scrappy

The real Jiggs is a brindle Catahoula — curious and a little scrappy, always first to check out whatever's around the next corner. Every block looks like a new adventure to him.

Glo

Warm, steady & kind

Glo is the reddish-brown one with the white chest blaze — warm, steady, and quick with encouragement. She's the friend who makes a big city feel a lot friendlier.

Book One — out now

Vote Yes for Hydrants

Something's off in the neighborhood, and Jiggs and Glo aren't about to sit still. With a magnifying glass, a stack of signs, and a few good friends, they set out to make their corner of the city a little better — one hydrant at a time.

It's a warm, funny story about friendship, standing up for your block, and finding a bit of bravery when it counts. Read it at bedtime; keep it on the shelf.
