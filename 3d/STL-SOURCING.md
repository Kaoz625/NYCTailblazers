# NYC Tailblazers 3D Studio — STL Sourcing Checklist

**Purpose:** every product on the storefront, and exactly where its STL comes from so you can legally print-and-sell it. This is a print-to-ship shop, so before a product goes "live" you need a file you're allowed to sell prints of.

**The one rule that keeps us safe:** *free to download ≠ free to sell.* Only sell prints from files that are (a) your own original/parametric design, (b) CC0 or CC-BY (credit the designer), or (c) covered by an active paid commercial license. **Never** sell prints of a CC-BY-**NC** file or an unmodified trademarked/fan-art model. As of 2026 Etsy actively removes listings using copied STLs — our custom-first approach is the durable, safe edge.

There are **three buckets.** Most of our best-margin line (place cards, tags, nameplates, lithophanes) is Bucket A — no file to buy, generated per order.

## Bucket B — FREE-FOR-COMMERCIAL (download & sell; verify CC0 or CC-BY per model)
Functional/decorative non-personalized goods with abundant CC0/CC-BY files. **Before printing:** open the model's license tab, confirm it says **CC0** (no credit needed) or **CC-BY** (credit the designer in the listing). Reject anything marked **-NC** (non-commercial) or fan-art of a trademarked character.

## Pricing model (from market research, so prices stay reasonable + profitable)
True cost = material + electricity + machine wear + machine-runtime rate ($2–4/hr) + labor ($20–35/hr handling; $35–60/hr design/paint) + post-processing (30–60% of labor) + packaging + 5–10% fail buffer. **Then markup 2.5–3.5× for stocked items, 3–5× for bespoke.** Sanity-check against the category's real retail band and round to a clean price.

Because we sell on our **own** storefront (no Etsy 10–13% + offsite-ad tax), we net ~45–60% at the same shelf price — use that edge to price at/below Etsy on the anchor line, not just to pocket margin. **Enforce a $12–15 order minimum** (or multipacks) on sub-$8 items; every job carries 15–30 min of fixed prep. Amortize any Bucket C subscription across expected monthly units.

Target margins: low-material (place cards, keychains, tags) 65–85% · medium FDM (dragons, organizers, vases) 55–70% · large (light boxes, big vases) 45–60%.
