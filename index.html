FAQ

Good questions, answered.

Are you insured and bonded?
Every walker is background-checked before their first walk. Reach out and we'll walk you through our coverage and safety practices so you feel completely comfortable handing over the leash.
How do you access my home?
Whatever you're comfortable with — a lockbox, a spare key, or a building doorman. We confirm your exact entry instructions at the meet & greet and treat your keys and home with total care.
What happens in bad weather?
Rain or shine, your dog still needs to go out. In extreme weather (heavy storms, dangerous heat or cold) we shorten the outdoor time and add indoor play, potty, and companionship instead — and we always tell you exactly what we did.
Do you walk on weekends and holidays?
Yes — we offer weekday and weekend availability. Holidays are available too and fill up fast, so book early. Just mention holiday dates in your booking.
Can you give my dog medication?
Yes, medication administration is available on drop-in visits. We'll go over exact dosing and instructions at the meet & greet and confirm everything in writing.
What if there's an emergency?
Your dog's safety comes first. We note your preferred vet and emergency contacts at the meet & greet, and we'll reach you immediately if anything comes up during a walk or visit.
How do I book my first walk?
Start with a free meet & greet — schedule it above or text us at (347) 260-8305. Once we've met your dog and you're happy, we'll set up your walks or visits.
