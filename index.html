The Heart of Tailblazers

NYC Tailblazers is more than a dog walking business. Dog walking is the foundation that helps fund a bigger long-term goal: supporting the raising and development of dogs that can one day serve veterans in need.

When you hire NYC Tailblazers, you support dependable local pet care and a mission built on change, hope, and new beginnings.
