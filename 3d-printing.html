The Process

How a custom print works.

Three simple steps from idea to object in hand.

01

Send Your Idea + Images

Tell us what you want made and attach any reference photos or sketches you have — the more detail, the better.

02

We Design & Quote

We turn your idea into a printable design and give you a clear quote before anything goes to print.

03

We Print & Hand It Over

Once you approve the design, we print your piece right here in NYC and get it to you.

No obligation to print: we design and quote first, so you know exactly what you're getting and what it costs before anything goes on the printer.
