Print-to-ship, step by step

How ordering works.

The same simple path whether you shop the catalog or commission something new.

STEP 01

Choose or describe it

Pick a product and your options (name, color, photo), or send your own idea with reference images for a custom quote.

STEP 02

We print it in NYC

Your piece is printed fresh, one layer at a time, in your chosen filament — then cleaned up and finished by hand.

STEP 03

It ships to your door

We pack it safely and mail it to you anywhere in the US. Custom pieces ship after you approve the design.

Honest, in-house pricing. Because we print and ship ourselves — no marketplace middleman — catalog prices are set fair and personalized items (place cards, keychains, nameplates, lithophanes) are made per order, so they’re one of a kind.
