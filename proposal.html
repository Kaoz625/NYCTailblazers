Dog Park Collaboration Proposal

NYCTailblazers x [Dog Park / Community Partner Name]

NYCTailblazers is a NYC-based dog community focused on safe, social, and organized dog-friendly experiences. We are seeking community-minded dog parks and partners to pilot responsible dog-owner events that increase engagement, goodwill, and neighborhood participation.

Collaboration Goals

  • Create recurring or one-off dog meetups and community events
  • Promote responsible dog ownership and park etiquette
  • Support local businesses, vendors, and neighborhood activity
  • Build a recognizable community partnership brand

Collaboration Options

  • Community Meetup Series: monthly or seasonal dog-owner meetups
  • Sponsored Event: co-hosted with local pet brands, trainers, groomers, or vendors
  • Educational Programming: behavior tips, etiquette reminders, pet wellness, and demos
  • Cleanup / Giveback Day: community-first neighborhood beautification events

What NYC Tailblazers Can Provide

  • Event concept and coordination support
  • Promotion through our website, social channels, and community network
  • Branded event-page or flyer copy
  • RSVP/check-in support
  • On-site host communication

What We Request From a Partner

  • Permission to collaborate and use the location/partner name in approved promotion
  • A point of contact
  • Approval on event format, schedule, and rules
  • Any site-specific operational requirements

Event Standards & Risk Controls

  • Dogs must remain under owner control at all times
  • Owners remain responsible for their dogs’ behavior, health, and actions
  • Aggressive or unsafe behavior is grounds for removal
  • Events are subject to weather, safety, and site conditions
  • Vaccination and licensing requirements should follow local law and park rules

Recommended Pilot

Start with one pilot event lasting 1.5–2 hours, then review turnout, operations, and fit before discussing recurring collaboration.

Next Step

We’d love to schedule a short call or email exchange to discuss a pilot event and partnership fit.
